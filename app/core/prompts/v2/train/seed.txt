You are generating training data for a machine learning model that detects payment intentions from chat messages.
The messages should simulate how real people talk about payments in natural, noisy, and diverse ways.

## Task Definition
Generate chat messages that belong to one of three intent categories:

- **payment_intent**
The mesasge sender declares they are about to send money or a note to make a payment to someone else.
Eg: “I’ll send you $5 later.” / “Pay Alice $20 in BTC.”

- **payment_request**:
The message sender requests, reminds, or demands money from someone else.

- **open_intent**:
Any arbitrary messages not related to any intent above but can include payment related context.

## Important Distinctions

1. Timing of payment
✅ payment_intent: The act of future or imminent payment or intent but no mentioned
- “I’ll transfer $50 in a bit.”
- "Send 40$"
❌ Not payment_intent if payment already completed
- “I sent $50 already.” → open_intent

2. Direction of Payment
- “Pay $10” → payment_intent
- “Give lisa 10msats” → payment_intent
- “Send me 10 eth” → payment_request

## Content Requirements
1. **Payment Amounts**: 
   - Creative in including the amount in the chat content, Ex: specifc, ambigious, no amount, ...

2. **Payment Methods**:
   - Be creative is mention crypt, currency, generical terms, or no mention,..
   - Include crypto currency (sats, msats, bitcoin, ...)
   - Include government-issued currency (USD, EUR, VND, $, ...)
   - Use slangs, symbol, formal, ...

3. **Language Styles and Tones**:
   - Diversity in language styles: format, casual, slang, command, ...
   - Ensure message length diversity: very-shot (less than 5 words), short, long (10 words), long-tail message, ...
   - Include 0-30% contextual noise
   - Include 0-30% typos, informal grammar

## Output Requirements
   - Each example must be unique — no repeated bigrams or patterns.
   - May include cryptographic/payment system details (msats, malformed invoices, fee caps, route hints).
   - JSON output format: {{ "msg": "mesasge", "label": "payment_intent" }}
   - valid labels: payment_request, payment_intent, open_intent
   - English only content.

Role play with given personas as the message sender
