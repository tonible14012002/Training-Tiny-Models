You are generating training data for a payment intention detection model.
The goal is to create chat messages that simulate how real humans express immediate payment intentions in chat.

## Task Definition
Generate examples that show different types of payment intentions in chat messages. The model needs to distinguish between:

- **payment_intent**: The user is declaring they will send money right now or in near future OR The user gives an imperative instruction to a system to execute a payment (e.g., “Pay Alice $20 now”).
- **payment_request**: The user request to receive money. (can be request, inform, force, remind, ...)
- **open_intent**: All arbitrary chat messages that are not related to any label above (no payment intent).

**Important Rules**
1. Do not generate messages where the money is already sent. Only intended, requested, or commanded payments are valid.
❌ Wrong: "Just sent you 3$" -> OPEN INTENT
✅ Correct: "I'll send you 3$" -> PAYMENT_SEND_INTENT
✅ Correct: "Give everyone 3$" -> PAYMENT_SEND_INTENT
✅ Correct: "Send me 3$" -> PAYMENT_REQUEST_INTENT

2. Do not generate long-term or delayed intent. Only immediate or short-term intent is valid.
❌ Wrong: “I’ll pay you enough on next year.”
✅ Correct: “I'll pay you tomorrow”

## Content Requirements
1. **Payment Amounts**: 
   - Creative in including the amount in the chat content, Ex: specifc, ambigious, no amount, ...

2. **Payment Methods**:
   - Be creative in mention crypto, currency, generical terms, or simply no mentioned,...

3. **Language Styles**:
   - Diversity in language styles: format, casual, slang, command, ...
   - Ensure message length diversity: very-shot (less than 5 words), short, long (10 words), long-tail message, ...

4. **Tones**
   - Diverse tones.
   - Add 0–30% contextual noise
   - Include 0-30% typos, informal grammar
