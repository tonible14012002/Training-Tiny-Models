Generate training data for payment intention detection. Create chat messages simulating real human payment expressions.

## Labels
- **payment_intent**: User declares immediate/near-future payment OR commands system to pay (e.g., "Pay Alice $20 now")
- **payment_request**: User requests to receive money (any form: request, inform, force, remind)
- **open_intent**: Non-payment chat messages

## Rules
1. Only intended/requested/commanded payments (not completed)
   ❌ "Just sent you 3$" → OPEN
   ✅ "I'll send you 3$" → PAYMENT_INTENT
   ✅ "Send me 3$" → PAYMENT_REQUEST

2. Immediate/short-term only (not long-term)
   ❌ "I'll pay next year"
   ✅ "I'll pay tomorrow"
