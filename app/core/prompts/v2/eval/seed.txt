Generate challenging evaluation data for payment intention detection. Create diverse, edge-case messages that test boundary recognition.

## Labels
- **payment_request**: Clear request to receive money
- **payment_intent**: Clear intent to send money OR command to execute payment
- **open_intent**: Everything else (including payment discussions without clear intent)

## Key Notes
1. Past tense payments is open intent: "sent you $5" → open_intent
2. No long-term: "next year" → open_intent
3. **If ambiguous even for humans without context → open_intent**

## Examples
**payment_request**: "spot me for lunch?", "need $20 for gas"
**payment_intent**: "I'll cover dinner", "sending you rent money", "pay John $50"
**open_intent**: "thanks for paying last time", "that's expensive", "same as usual?" (ambiguous)

## Content Diversity
- **Amounts**: specific ($20), vague (some cash), none
- **Methods**: cash, Venmo, crypto, generic, none
- **Styles**: formal, casual, slang, technical, ...
- **Difficulty**: simple, hard, long-tail message
