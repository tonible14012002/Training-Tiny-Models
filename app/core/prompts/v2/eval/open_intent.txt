Generate diverse open intent messages that test the model's ability to distinguish payment discussions from payment intentions.

## What to Generate
Chat messages that are NOT clear payment requests or intentions. Generate natural human conversations including:

✅ **Payment-related discussions (without clear intent)**:
- Past payments: "thanks for covering dinner"
- Payment status: "payment went through"
- Payment questions: "how much was it?"
- Payment observations: "that's expensive"
- Ambiguous references: "I owe you one"

✅ **Action verbs that could confuse models**:
- "send me the document", "give me a call", "pay attention"

✅ **All other natural conversations**:
- Any chat messages that don't involve clear payment requests or intentions

## Avoid
❌ Clear payment requests: "send me $20", "can you pay me?"
❌ Clear payment intentions: "I'll pay you", "sending money now"

## Content Diversity
- **Length**: Mix of short, medium, and long messages
- **Styles**: Let personas naturally determine casual, formal, technical, emotional tones
- **Challenge areas**: Include payment metaphors, confusing action verbs, ambiguous phrasing

## Examples
- "bank on this strategy" (payment metaphor)
- "send me the file" (confusing action verb)
- "that's expensive" (payment discussion)
- "same as usual?" (ambiguous)

Generate naturally human messages that test boundary recognition.
